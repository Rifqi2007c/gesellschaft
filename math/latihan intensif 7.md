mean($\bar x$/$\mu$) = 45
sisihan piawai($\sigma$) = 3.5

formula:
$$Z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$$

#### a) lebih daripada 51kg | $Z>51kg$
1. kira skor
$$Z= \frac{51-45}{3.5} = 1.71$$
2. cari kebarangkalian | $Kb(Z>1.71)$
$$1-Kb(Z>1.71)$$
berdasarkan [[jadual-taburan-normal-piawai.jpg]] > 1.71 = 0.9564
$$1-0.9564 = 0.0436$$

#### b) Antara 40kg dan 50kg | $(40kg < Z < 50kg)$
1. kira skor
$$Z_1=\frac{40-45}{3.5} = -1.43$$
$$Z_1 = \frac{50-45}{3.5} = 1.43$$
2. cari kebarangkalian | $Kb(-1.43 < Z < 1.43)$
$$Kb(Z<-1.43)-Kb(Z<1.43)$$
berdasarkan [[jadual-taburan-normal-piawai.jpg]] > $1.43=0.9236$
$$Kb(Z<-1.43)=1-0.9236=0.0764$$
$$Kb(Z<1.43) = 0.9236$$
$$0.9236-0.0764=0.8472$$
### c) kurang daripada 41kg | $Z<41$
1. kira skor
$$Z=\frac{41-45}{3.5}=-1.14$$
2. cari kebarangkalian | $Kb(Z<-1.14)$
$$Kb(Z<-1.14)$$
berdasarkan [[jadual-taburan-normal-piawai.jpg]] > 1.14 = 0.8729
$$1-0.8729=0.1271$$

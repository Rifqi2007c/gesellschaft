formula data tak terkumpul
$$
\frac {\overline{x} = \Sigma x_1} {n}
$$

contoh:
data: 5,3,8,12,18,20,24,25

formula data terkumpul
$$
\overline{x} = \frac {\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n} f_i x_i} {\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n} f_i}
$$
contoh:
data:

| 700-799   | 8   |
| --------- | --- |
| 800-899   | 13  |
| 900-999   | 14  |
| 1000-1099 | 10  |
| 1100-1199 | 25  |
| 1200-1299 | 4   |
- add the two number then divide by 2 in the first row
	- $700+799=1499$ then $1499 \div 2=749.5$
- make new table to solve

| $x_i$  | $f_i$ | $f_ix_i$ |
| ------ | ----- | -------- |
| 749.5  | 8     | 5996     |
| 849.5  | 13    | 11043.5  |
| 949.5  | 14    | 13293    |
| 1049.5 | 10    | 10495    |
| 1149.5 | 25    | 28737.5  |
| 1249.5 | 4     | 4998     |
- multiply $x_i$ with $f_i$
	- $749.5 \times 8 = 5996$
$$
\overline{x} = \frac {\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n} f_i x_i} {\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n} f_i} = \frac{74563}{74} = 1007.6081
$$

### find median from ungroup data (odd)
example:
data: 45,38,48,25,27,40,24

- rearange from low to high
	- 24,25,27,38,40,45,48
- find median
$$
\frac{n+1}{2} = \frac{7+1}{2} = 4
$$
$\Large n=$ data total

answer: 38
38 is a median because its at the 4th data in the ungroup data
